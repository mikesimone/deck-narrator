Covers everything since 1.1.0 (1.2.0 and 1.2.1).

**Download:** `deck-narrator-1.2.1-x64.msi` is the installer: it includes the voices, runs offline and replaces any older version. `deck-narrator-windows-x64.zip` is the no-install copy.

## New in 1.2.1

- **Check pronunciation.** Finds only the words that might be read wrong: words with more than one pronunciation (read, lead, record, live), words the voice doesn't know, and all-caps names it would spell out (like NGINX). Each has a **Play** button for every option, plus a "Sounds like" box. **Use this** writes the fix into the slide's speaker notes, leaves the formatting alone, and keeps a one-time backup, `<deck> (before deck-narrator).pptx`. From the command line: `--check-pronunciation` and `--fix-word`.
- **Instant voice samples.** The installer comes with the default greeting already recorded for every English voice, so **Play sample** starts right away. Any other sample you play is saved, and replays instantly the next time (the newest 200 are kept).
- **Updates.** At startup the app checks GitHub once a day for a newer version. If there is one, an **Update** button downloads the installer, checks it against GitHub's checksum, installs it and reopens. You can turn this off on the Options tab, or press **Check now**.

## New in 1.2.0

- **Uses PowerPoint if you have it.** Slides are drawn with Microsoft PowerPoint when it's installed, so LibreOffice isn't needed. Choose with `--renderer auto|powerpoint|libreoffice`.
- **LibreOffice only when it's needed, and it leaves Office alone.** The installer (or the GUI's **Install LibreOffice** button) downloads LibreOffice from The Document Foundation and checks its signature. It won't take over .pptx, .docx or .xlsx files, and it doesn't add a desktop icon. winget isn't required.
- **ffmpeg is built in.**
- **Voice updates.** New and updated voices are checked for once a day, or on demand with `--update-voices`.
- **New look.** Settings are grouped into tabs (Deck, Voice, Pronunciation, Options, Log), and the window fits a 1024x768 screen.
