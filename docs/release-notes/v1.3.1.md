**Download:** on Windows, `deck-narrator-1.3.1-x64.msi` is the installer. It includes the voices, runs offline and replaces any older version, and 1.2.1 or newer offers this update on its own the next time it starts. On a Mac with Apple Silicon, use `deck-narrator-1.3.1-mac-arm64.dmg` and follow the [Mac guide](https://github.com/mikesimone/deck-narrator/blob/main/docs/MAC.md). `deck-narrator-windows-x64.zip` is the no-install Windows copy.

## Fixed in 1.3.1

- **Your own voice uses your NVIDIA graphics card.** 1.3.0 ran it on the processor even when there was a card, which made it many times slower. **Settings > Your own voice** now says which one it uses.
- **No more words cut off at the end.** Chatterbox sometimes stopped a word early. Sentences now end cleanly, and each one is listened to as it's made: one that was cut short is made again. The first time you use your voice after updating, it downloads the small checker (about 200 MB).
- **Smoother joins between sentences.** Short fades, and a quiet room tone in the gaps instead of dead silence.

## New

- **Progress for your own voice:** the log counts the sentences and estimates the time left, and **Stop** now stops it right away on a Mac too.
- **Voice packs can carry a second, clean recording** for the sound of the voice, while the main one still sets how it speaks, and can set the pace: `pause_scale` for longer pauses and `speed` to slow it down without changing the pitch. Your own voice also leaves a little more room between sentences. See [Voice packs](https://github.com/mikesimone/deck-narrator/blob/main/docs/OWN-VOICE.md#voice-packs).
- **A new voice's sample is made when you add it** and kept, so **Play sample** is instant afterwards.
- **Check pronunciation reuses clips:** every word listed in one sentence shares the clip of that sentence as it's said now, and the clip of the way you pick with **Use this** is kept.
- **Remove Chatterbox...** in Settings frees its 4 to 6 GB and keeps your voices. Setup checks for 10 GB of free space before it starts.
