**Download:** on Windows, `deck-narrator-1.3.2-x64.msi` is the installer. It includes the voices, runs offline and replaces any older version, and 1.2.1 or newer offers this update on its own the next time it starts. On a Mac with Apple Silicon, use `deck-narrator-1.3.2-mac-arm64.dmg` and follow the [Mac guide](https://github.com/mikesimone/deck-narrator/blob/main/docs/MAC.md). `deck-narrator-windows-x64.zip` is the no-install Windows copy.

## New in 1.3.2

- **Voice packs can carry a fine-tuned S3Gen** (`s3gen.safetensors`, named in `voice.json` as `"s3gen"`), the part of Chatterbox that turns speech into sound, so a voice trained further sounds more like the person. Packs without it work as before. See [Voice packs](https://github.com/mikesimone/deck-narrator/blob/main/docs/OWN-VOICE.md#voice-packs).
