**Download:** on Windows, `deck-narrator-1.3.0-x64.msi` is the installer. It includes the voices, runs offline and replaces any older version, and 1.2.1 or newer offers this update on its own the next time it starts. With Chocolatey, run `choco upgrade deck-narrator`. On a Mac with Apple Silicon, use `deck-narrator-1.3.0-mac-arm64.dmg` and follow the [Mac guide](https://github.com/mikesimone/deck-narrator/blob/main/docs/MAC.md). `deck-narrator-windows-x64.zip` is the no-install Windows copy.

## New in 1.3.0

- **Narrate in your own voice.** Record about 20 seconds of yourself talking, add it in **Settings > Your own voice**, and pick it in the **Voice** list like any other voice. It also works with `[voice: name]` in the notes, so your voice and the built-in ones can share a deck. It uses [Chatterbox](https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox), a free voice model from Resemble AI, and everything stays on your computer.
- **Optional and set up once.** Your own voice is a separate 4 to 6 GB download, so the normal install stays the same size. Click **Set up** in Settings, or, on Windows with PowerPoint or LibreOffice already installed, tick **Set up your own voice now** on the installer's last page.
- **Voice packs.** If you fine-tuned Chatterbox on a voice yourself, add the `.zip` (a recording, plus your `t3.safetensors` and settings) the same way.
- **Steady results.** The same notes always come out the same, so remaking a video doesn't change the slides you didn't touch.

It's much slower than the built-in voices without an NVIDIA graphics card, so try one slide first. The [Your own voice guide](https://github.com/mikesimone/deck-narrator/blob/main/docs/OWN-VOICE.md) has the details.
