**Download:** on Windows, `deck-narrator-1.3.4-x64.msi` is the installer. It includes the voices, runs offline and replaces any older version, and 1.2.1 or newer offers this update on its own the next time it starts. On a Mac with Apple Silicon, use `deck-narrator-1.3.4-mac-arm64.dmg` and follow the [Mac guide](https://github.com/mikesimone/deck-narrator/blob/main/docs/MAC.md). `deck-narrator-windows-x64.zip` is the no-install Windows copy.

## New in 1.3.4

- **The pronunciation dictionary works for your own voice.** Your own voice reads spelling, not phonemes, so a dictionary entry can now carry a respelling for it: `Ent=[Ennt](/ˈɛnt/)` makes the built-in voices use the phonemes and your voice say "Ennt". Save one in the **Pronunciation** tab (**Your own voice says** Ent **as** Ennt, **Save**) or with `--say-as Ent Ennt`; any phonemes the word already had are kept, and **Use this** later keeps the respelling. See [Pronunciation](https://github.com/mikesimone/deck-narrator/blob/main/docs/PRONUNCIATION.md#the-pronunciation-file-every-occurrence).
