**Download:** on Windows, `deck-narrator-1.3.3-x64.msi` is the installer. It includes the voices, runs offline and replaces any older version, and 1.2.1 or newer offers this update on its own the next time it starts. On a Mac with Apple Silicon, use `deck-narrator-1.3.3-mac-arm64.dmg` and follow the [Mac guide](https://github.com/mikesimone/deck-narrator/blob/main/docs/MAC.md). `deck-narrator-windows-x64.zip` is the no-install Windows copy.

## New in 1.3.3

- **Cleaner own voice.** Two new optional voice pack settings in `voice.json`: `"depop": true` removes the low thud a trained voice can put on a word after a pause, and `"breath_db": -9` makes breaths quieter (and trims long ones) without touching `s`, `sh`, `f` or `h`. Packs without them sound as before. See [Voice packs](https://github.com/mikesimone/deck-narrator/blob/main/docs/OWN-VOICE.md#voice-packs).
- **Train your own voice.** The optional [voice-training](https://github.com/mikesimone/deck-narrator/tree/main/voice-training) folder turns your own recordings into a voice pack (needs an NVIDIA card with 12 GB or more; not part of the installer).
